Epson tools
===========
Put official Epson utilities here (download them from Epson's own support site):
  - Epson Scan 2, Epson Printer Utility / driver installers
  - Epson Connect and model-specific drivers

How it works: the toolkit's "Epson" button lists runnable files in this folder
(.exe, .msi, .bat, .cmd, .lnk, .ps1; sub-folders up to 2 levels). With nothing here,
it opens the Epson support site instead.

Nozzle check, head cleaning, power cleaning and ink charging are in the driver's
Maintenance tab (use "Printer preferences / maintenance" in the toolkit) or in the
printer's own menu - they depend on the model. Waste-ink / maintenance-counter reset
tools are model-specific and should only be used from the manufacturer or authorised
service sources.
Respect Epson's licence terms when copying installers to a USB drive.
