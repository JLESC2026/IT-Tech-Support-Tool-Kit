Brother tools
=============
Put official Brother utilities here (download them from Brother's own support site):
  - Brother Utilities / iPrint&Scan installers
  - Brother Driver Deployment Wizard, model-specific drivers

How it works: the toolkit's "Brother" button lists runnable files in this folder
(.exe, .msi, .bat, .cmd, .lnk, .ps1; sub-folders up to 2 levels). With nothing here,
it opens the Brother support site instead.

Cleaning and calibration options are in the printer's own menu, Brother Utilities, or
the driver's Maintenance tab (use "Printer preferences / maintenance" in the toolkit) -
they depend on the model.
Respect Brother's licence terms when copying installers to a USB drive.
