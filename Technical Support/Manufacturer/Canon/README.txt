Canon tools
===========
Put official Canon utilities here (download them from Canon's own support site):
  - Canon IJ Network Tool / IJ Scan Utility installers
  - Model-specific printer drivers and MP Drivers

How it works: the toolkit's "Canon" button lists runnable files in this folder
(.exe, .msi, .bat, .cmd, .lnk, .ps1; sub-folders up to 2 levels). With nothing here,
it opens the Canon support site instead.

Cleaning, deep cleaning, nozzle check and similar functions are in the driver's
Maintenance tab (use "Printer preferences / maintenance" in the toolkit) or in the
printer's own menu - they depend on the model.
Respect Canon's licence terms when copying installers to a USB drive.
