HP tools
========
Put official HP utilities here (download them from HP's own support site):
  - HP Print and Scan Doctor (diagnoses printing and scanning problems)
  - HP Smart installer, HP Universal Print Driver, model-specific drivers

How it works: the toolkit's "HP" button lists runnable files in this folder
(.exe, .msi, .bat, .cmd, .lnk, .ps1; sub-folders up to 2 levels). With nothing here,
it opens the HP support site instead.

Printhead / cartridge maintenance on HP printers is done in HP Smart, the printer's
own control panel, or the "Printer Toolbox" in the driver - it is model specific.
Respect HP's licence terms when copying installers to a USB drive.
